# Frank (1965), Eqs. (18)–(22): three- and four-index symbols of the conventional hexagonal cell

Source: F. C. Frank, "On Miller–Bravais indices and four-dimensional vectors", _Acta Cryst._ **18**,
862–866 (1965), doi:10.1107/S0365110X65002116, section "Interconversion of coordinates", printed
p. 864, Eqs. (18)–(22). Transcribed on 2026-11-25 from the PDF in Thomas' Zotero (item
`JUFIBJSH`, attachment `HCKTL6NT`; the PDF page number is the printed page − 861), read from the
page image at 120 dpi.

Frank writes a direction with square brackets and a plane with round ones, and uses the same
letters for both ("the shape of the brackets must generally suffice to distinguish planes from
directions", p. 863). A four-index symbol [hkil] of a direction is the four-vector of Weber's
symbol [uvtw] (p. 864, "the direction symbolized as [uvtw] is that of the cartesian 4-vector
[u, v, t, λw]"). The correspondences hold "apart from a constant multiplying factor of no
importance" (p. 864), for "the conventional hexagonal unit cell, with the three basis vectors"
(Eq. (18)):

- a₁ = [2, −1, −1, 0] 6^(−1/2) a,
- a₂ = [−1, 2, −1, 0] 6^(−1/2) a,
- c = [0, 0, 0, 1] c = [0, 0, 0, 3λ] 6^(−1/2) a,

so a₁, a₂ are the basal axes at 120° and c the hexagonal axis, the cell a, b, c of ITA's hexagonal
axes.

Transcription conventions: one row per equation; "From" lists the letters of the symbol on the
left, "To" the components on the right, separated by `;`, each a linear expression in those
letters as printed (`2s-t` for 2s − t).

| Eq. | Kind | From | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | direction, four indices to three | h k i l | h-i; k-i; l |
| 20 | direction, three indices to four | s t u | 2s-t; -s+2t; -s-t; 3u |
| 21 | plane, four indices to three | h k i l | h; k; l |
| 22 | plane, three indices to four | s t u | s; t; -s-t; u |

Eq. (22) is printed as "(stu)→[st(−s−t)u]", with a square bracket on the right, which elsewhere
marks a direction; the row is in the paragraph "and for planes" and maps a plane (stu), so the
bracket is read as round. The components are as printed.
