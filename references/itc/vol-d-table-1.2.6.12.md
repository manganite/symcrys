# ITC Vol. D Table 1.2.6.12: Magnetic point groups

Source: _International Tables for Crystallography_, Vol. D, 2nd edition (2013), Chapter 1.2
(Representations of crystallographic groups; T. Janssen), Table 1.2.6.12, printed p. 66.

Transcribed on 2026-11-26 from the page image at 450 dpi, primes and overbars as printed.

What the table covers: the 122 magnetic point groups by crystal class, one row per class: type I
the class, type II its grey group, type III its black-and-white groups. The symbols name the groups
without fixing an orientation of the axes; several are written in another orientation than the
keys of symcrys (`2mm`, `-62m`, `m'mm`).

Transcription conventions:

- A leading minus sign is an overbar (`-4`, `-6'`); a prime `'` is the printed prime.
- Type III: the symbols of a row as printed, separated by `, `. The print ends the row of 2/m with a
  comma after `2'/m'`; it is left out here.

Deviations from Table 1.5.2.3 (pp. 111–112), which lists the 90 groups of types I and III with all
their symbols and operators and is the reference for their symbols:

- Erratum: the row of 222 prints `2'2'` for the group Table 1.5.2.3 writes `2'2'2` (D2(C2)). Kept
  as printed; the tests read the corrected symbol.
- The cubic groups with a primed first mirror are printed without the prime on the 3 with overbar
  (`m'-3`, `m'-3m`, `m'-3m'`, where Table 1.5.2.3 writes `m'-3'`, `m'-3'm`, `m'-3'm'`), as Birss
  writes them without the overbar (`m'3m`). Kept as printed; this spelling is in common use.
