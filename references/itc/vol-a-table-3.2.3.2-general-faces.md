# ITA Table 3.2.3.2: faces of the general form, hexagonal axes

Source: _International Tables for Crystallography_, Vol. A, 6th edition (2016), Chapter 3.2 (Point
groups and crystal classes), Table 3.2.3.2, printed pp. 752–764. Transcribed 2026-11-21 from the
page images of the chapter PDF (Zotero item `7NZ258UB`, attachment `Y6Z8P8NR`, PDF pages 33–45, at
200 dpi), overbars included.

The last column of the first row of each entry, the Miller indices of the faces of the general
form, for the 16 entries with hexagonal axes (the trigonal classes with hexagonal axes and the
hexagonal classes): in these the lattice basis is not orthonormal, so a face transforms otherwise
than a lattice direction. The faces are listed in printed order, row by row and left to right; the
general positions of the same entries are in `vol-a-table-3.2.3.2-general-positions.md`. Not
transcribed: the limiting forms and the special positions, and the entries of the other systems.

Order. In 14 entries the faces follow the coordinate triplets of the general position, the face
of an operation beside its triplet. In 6/m (p. 760) and 6mm (p. 762) the third and fourth rows
of faces are printed in the other order: beside x̄,ȳ,z̄ stand (hkil̄) (ihkl̄) (kihl̄), the faces of
x,y,z̄, and in 6mm beside ȳ,x̄,z stand (khil) (hikl) (ikhl), the faces of y,x,z. Transcribed as
printed.

Transcription conventions: Bravais–Miller indices (hkil) as printed, four components separated by
commas, a leading minus sign for an overbar (`-k,-h,-i,-l` for (k̄h̄īl̄)); faces separated by
spaces.
