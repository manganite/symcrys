# ITA Table 3.2.3.2: faces of the general form, hexagonal and rhombohedral axes

Source: _International Tables for Crystallography_, Vol. A, 6th edition (2016), Chapter 3.2 (Point
groups and crystal classes), Table 3.2.3.2, printed pp. 752–764. Transcribed 2026-11-21 from the
page images of the chapter PDF (Zotero item `7NZ258UB`, attachment `Y6Z8P8NR`, PDF pages 33–45, at
200 dpi), overbars included. The five entries with rhombohedral axes (3, -3, 32, 3m, -3m;
pp. 753, 753, 755, 756, 759) were added 2026-11-23 from the page images of the same PDF (PDF pages
34, 36, 37, 40; printed page = PDF page + 719), read at 300–500 dpi.

The last column of the first row of each entry, the Miller indices of the faces of the general
form, for the 21 entries with hexagonal or rhombohedral axes (the trigonal classes with both kinds
of axes and the hexagonal classes): in these the lattice basis is not orthonormal, so a face
transforms otherwise than a lattice direction. The faces are listed in printed order, row by row
and left to right; the general positions of the same entries are in
`vol-a-table-3.2.3.2-general-positions.md`. Not transcribed: the limiting forms and the special
positions, and the entries of the other systems.

Order. In 16 entries the faces follow the coordinate triplets of the general position, the face
of an operation beside its triplet. In 6/m (p. 760) and 6mm (p. 762) the third and fourth rows
of faces are printed in the other order: beside x̄,ȳ,z̄ stand (hkil̄) (ihkl̄) (kihl̄), the faces of
x,y,z̄, and in 6mm beside ȳ,x̄,z stand (khil) (hikl) (ikhl), the faces of y,x,z. In 32, 3m and -3m
with rhombohedral axes (pp. 755, 756, 759) the row of faces of the coset of the twofold rotations
or reflections is printed in a cyclically shifted order: beside z̄,ȳ,x̄ ȳ,x̄,z̄ x̄,z̄,ȳ (32, -3m)
stand (k̄h̄l̄) (h̄l̄k̄) (l̄k̄h̄), the faces of ȳ,x̄,z̄ x̄,z̄,ȳ z̄,ȳ,x̄, and beside z,y,x y,x,z x,z,y
(3m, -3m) stand (khl) (hlk) (lkh), the faces of y,x,z x,z,y z,y,x. Transcribed as printed.

Transcription conventions: Bravais–Miller indices (hkil) as printed, four components separated by
commas, a leading minus sign for an overbar (`-k,-h,-i,-l` for (k̄h̄īl̄)); for rhombohedral axes
Miller indices (hkl), three components; faces separated by spaces.
