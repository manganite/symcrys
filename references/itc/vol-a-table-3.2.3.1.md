# ITA Table 3.2.3.1: the ten two-dimensional crystallographic point groups

Source: _International Tables for Crystallography_, Vol. A, 6th edition (2016), Chapter 3.2 (Point
groups and crystal classes; H. Klapper, Th. Hahn and M. I. Aroyo, § 3.2.3), Table 3.2.3.1, printed
pp. 742–744. Transcribed on 2026-11-26; overbars as printed.

The table lists, for each plane point group in each orientation it prints (the headline symbol:
`3m1` and `31m` are the two orientations of 3m), the Wyckoff positions (columns 1 to 4:
multiplicity, Wyckoff letter, oriented site-symmetry symbol, coordinate doublets) and under the
stereographic projections the edge forms (roman type) with the point forms (italic) below them; of
two entries the second is a limiting (noncharacteristic) form. The last column gives the Miller
indices of the equivalent edges, Bravais–Miller indices (hki) for the hexagonal groups.

Transcription conventions: a leading `-` is an overbar; coordinate doublets separated by `;`,
their two coordinates by `,`; Miller indices with their components separated by `,`, the edges of a
form by `;`, as printed in rows; where print offers a second set after "or", it follows in the
column "Or". "Form" is `basic` for the first entry of a position and `limiting` for the second.
