# ITC Vol. D Table 1.5.2.3: The 90 magnetic point groups of types 2 and 3

Source: _International Tables for Crystallography_, Vol. D, 2nd edition (2013), Chapter 1.5
(Magnetic properties; A. S. Borovik-Romanov, H. Grimmer and M. Kenzelmann), Table 1.5.2.3, printed
pp. 111–112 (triclinic to tetragonal on p. 111, trigonal to cubic on p. 112).

Transcribed on 2026-11-19 from the page images of the volume in Thomas' Zotero (the text layer of
the PDF loses primes, overbars and underlines, so it was not used).

What the table covers: the chapter numbers the types of magnetic point groups differently from the
usual I, II, III. Its first type are the 32 grey groups (§ 1.5.2.1, eq. (1.5.2.1)), its second type
the 32 crystallographic point groups without time reversal, its third type the 58 black-and-white
groups. The table lists the second and the third type: 32 + 58 = 90 groups. The grey groups are not
listed; each is a crystallographic point group P together with all its operations multiplied by
time reversal (eq. (1.5.2.1)).

Transcription conventions:

- A leading minus sign is an overbar (`-4`, `-1`); a prime `'` is the printed prime (time
  reversal). Overbar and prime together: `-4'`.
- Shubnikov column: the print underlines the elements combined with time reversal; an underlined
  element is written with a leading underscore (`_2`, `_m`, `_-6` for an underlined 6 with
  overbar). `.` is the printed centred dot, `:` and `/` as printed.
- Full symbols: the positions are separated by single spaces; a printed fraction is written with a
  slash (`2'/m`).
- Operators: subscripts after an underscore (`2_x`, `m_xy`, `2'_-xy`, `-4'_z`); `±` as printed;
  `n(op)` as printed for n equivalent operations, `⊥` for the printed subscript ⊥ (perpendicular to
  the principal axis); in the cubic system the print gives no subscripts.
- Schoenflies symbols: subscripts written inline (`C2h(Ci)`, `D3d(S6)`).
