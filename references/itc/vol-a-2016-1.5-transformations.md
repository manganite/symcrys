# ITA Chapter 1.5: worked transformations of coordinate systems

Source: _International Tables for Crystallography_, Vol. A, 6th edition (2016), Chapter 1.5
(Transformations of coordinate systems; H. Wondratschek, M. I. Aroyo, B. Souvignier and
G. Chapuis), printed pp. 75–90 (PDF page = printed page − 75). Transcribed on 2026-11-25 from the
page images of the chapter PDF in Thomas' Zotero (attachment `AHHQY85L`), rendered at 130 dpi (the
zircon coordinates at 300 dpi), and checked again value by value at 250–500 dpi in the review of
PR #173; the text layer of the PDF takes fractions apart, so it was not used.

Each example gives the transformation (P, p) as printed, the quantities it transforms and the
results as printed. Transcription conventions: a matrix is written by rows, rows separated by `;`,
entries by spaces (`0 1/2 1/2; 1/2 0 1/2; 1/2 1/2 0`); a column of coordinates or a vector as its
entries separated by spaces; a leading `-` is an overbar or a minus sign; fractions as `n/d`. An
operation is the pair `W | w`. Where print gives normalized coordinates (0 ≤ xᵢ < 1), the note
says so; equations are cited with their printed numbers.

## 2 — Zircon, origin shift, pp. 75–76

1/8) from the origin choice 1 values, i.e. leave the x coordinate unchanged, add 1/4 = 0.25 to the
